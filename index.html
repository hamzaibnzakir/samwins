The system

Three stages from first product to scaled store.

Most courses hand you videos and leave. Samwins takes you through each stage with a mentor who looks at your numbers and tells you what to do next.

1

Launch

Pick a product with real demand and build a store that converts before you spend on ads.

  • Product research with clear pass/fail criteria
  • Store build, offer and pricing
  • Supplier and shipping setup
2

Test

Run Meta ads with a fixed testing budget and clear rules for what to cut and what to keep.

  • Creative angles and hooks that stop the scroll
  • Campaign structure we use on our own stores
  • Weekly reviews of your ad numbers
3

Scale

Push budget into winners, protect your margins and put systems in place so the store runs without you on every order.

  • Scaling playbook for winning ad sets
  • Fulfilment and customer service flow
  • Profit tracking you can trust

It's not for you if
